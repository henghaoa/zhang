## 战斗动漫专属指令

```
CRITICAL STYLE - BATTLE ANIME:
- Anime art style similar to Demon Slayer, Jujutsu Kaisen, or Naruto
- Dynamic action: speed lines, motion blur, impact frames
- Energy effects: glowing auras, fire/lightning/wind elemental powers
- Dramatic camera: extreme angles, dutch tilt, explosive zoom
- Color: high contrast, saturated, cel-shading look
- Include: shockwave rings, particle effects, dramatic dust clouds
```
